Signals are research queues, not recommendations. PEAD (post-earnings drift) = latest quarter's YoY PAT ≥50% and operating profit ≥30% and sales ≥10%, at PE ≤70 and mcap ≤₹1,00,000 Cr; 🚀 confirmed adds a weekly technical breakout near 52-week highs. Patterns: P1 = OPM up ≥3pts vs 3y, quarterly trend intact · P3 building = capex ≥40% of 2-yr-ago gross block, margins not yet expanded · P3 inflecting = TTM EBITDA growing ≥1.4× sales · P5 = borrowings down ≥15% over 2y · ⚠ rev = reverse operating leverage. Technicals (weekly closes, 20W & 40W moving averages, with 52-week high/low and swing pivots): entry-zone = uptrend, price near 20WMA support · uptrend = hold, add on pullback · extended = >25% above 20WMA, wait · exit-signal = weekly close below 20WMA · breakdown = below 40WMA · 🚀 = within 5% of 52w high.
